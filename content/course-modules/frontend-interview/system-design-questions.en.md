Frontend system design questions: how to design a frontend system for specific requirements. There is no single right answer to the whole task, so in an interview first clarify the requirements and constraints, then explain your decisions and their trade-offs. Only the scenarios the earlier modules don't cover are here. Try to answer each one out loud, then check yourself in the quiz after this slide: each question here has one matching quiz question.

1. How do you design a frontend that switches seamlessly between online and offline modes?
2. How do you architect internationalization (i18n) and localization (L10n)?
3. How do you integrate a frontend with a microservices backend, or a legacy backend with inconsistent, slow APIs?
4. How do you design pagination and infinite scrolling for 100,000+ records?
5. How do you prepare a frontend for traffic peaks such as Black Friday?
6. How do you add push notifications to a PWA?
7. How do you design state for a complex UI with interrelated data?
8. How do you work with rate-limited or unreliable APIs without hurting the user experience?
9. How do you implement optimistic UI updates while keeping data consistent?
10. How do you design a system of feature flags and A/B tests?
11. How do you work with a backend team using a contract-first approach (OpenAPI, GraphQL schemas)?
12. How do you support several environments (dev, staging, prod) with different configurations?
13. How do you deploy a frontend with zero downtime?
14. How do you design a frontend that recovers from serious failures, such as corrupted saved state?
