A list of questions on HTML itself, split into three levels. Only the ones the earlier modules don't cover are here: mostly specific tags and attributes for forms, media and metadata. Try to answer each one out loud, then check yourself in the quizzes after this slide: each question here has one matching quiz question.

## Beginner

1. What is the difference between HTML and HTML5?
2. What is the difference between a tag and an element?
3. What is the difference between `<section>`, `<article>` and `<div>`?
4. How do you create a hyperlink? How do absolute and relative URLs differ?
5. What does a form's `action` attribute do, and what happens if you leave it out?
6. How do you link to an email address or a phone number?
7. How do you build a group of checkboxes? What are `<fieldset>` and `<legend>` for?
8. What is the `<label>` tag, and how does the `for`/`id` pair improve accessibility?
9. How do you build a dropdown list?
10. What is the `<blockquote>` tag for, and when do you use `cite`?

## Intermediate

11. How do you add video or audio in HTML5? Which formats are supported?
12. What does `<noscript>` do, and why is it still relevant?
13. How do you make a multi-line text input?
14. What is the `<base>` tag?
15. What is `enctype` in forms? When do you need `multipart/form-data`?
16. How do you create a hidden input field?
17. What is the `<address>` tag for?
18. What is the `lang` attribute on `<html>` for?
19. What is the `contenteditable` attribute?
20. What are HTML entities, and how do you insert special characters?
21. What is `<canvas>`, and how does it differ from SVG?
22. How do you get the user's location with the Geolocation API?

## Advanced

23. What is the `<template>` tag for?
24. What is the `rel` attribute on `<link>` for?
25. How do you mark up the language versions of a page?
26. What is the `<output>` tag?
27. What is the `<datalist>` tag?
28. What is the difference between `defer` and `async` on `<script>`?
29. How do you control the loading priority of images (`loading`, `fetchpriority`, `preload`)?
30. What is the `<picture>` element?
31. What is the `tabindex` attribute?
32. What are HTML global attributes? Give examples.

## HTML in React apps

33. How do `data-*` and `aria-*` attributes behave in JSX compared with other props?
34. How do you build an accessible multi-level navigation menu?
35. How do HTML parsing and rendering affect load speed? Why does DOM size matter?
36. How do you design HTML for progressive enhancement in a React app?
37. What is the role of HTML in a server-rendered React app? What causes hydration errors?
38. How do you manage page metadata (`<title>`, `<meta>`) in a single-page app?
