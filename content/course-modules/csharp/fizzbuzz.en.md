The classic. Print the numbers from 1 to 15, one per line, but:

- print `Fizz` instead of numbers divisible by 3,
- print `Buzz` instead of numbers divisible by 5,
- print `FizzBuzz` instead of numbers divisible by both.

The output starts like this:

```text
1
2
Fizz
4
Buzz
```

and its last line is `FizzBuzz`.
