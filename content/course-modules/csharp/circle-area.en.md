A circle has radius `5`. Print its area (`π · r²`) and its circumference (`2 · π · r`), each **rounded to two decimal places**, in this format:

```text
Area: 78.54
Circumference: 31.42
```

Use `Math.PI` for π, and `Math.Round(value, 2)` for rounding. Keep the radius in a variable so that changing it changes both results.
