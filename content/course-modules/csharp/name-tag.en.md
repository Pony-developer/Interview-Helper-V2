You have a first name and a last name typed by someone in a hurry: extra spaces and the wrong capital letters. Clean them up and print a name tag in exactly this format:

```text
Name: Ada Lovelace
Initials: A.L.
Length: 12
Born: 1815
```

- Remove the spaces around the first name.
- Make only the **first** letter of each name a capital and the rest lower case.
- `Length` is the length of the full name, including the space between the two names.
- `Initials` are the first letters of both names, each followed by a dot.

Remember that strings are immutable: store the result of every method call.
