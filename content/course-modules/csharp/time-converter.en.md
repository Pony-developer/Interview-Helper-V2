The variable `totalSeconds` holds a duration. Print it as hours, minutes and seconds in this exact format:

```text
27h 26m 5s
```

Use `/` and `%` to calculate the parts, and store each one in a variable before printing. Do not type the answer in directly: the program has to work out the numbers from `totalSeconds`.

Hint: an hour is 3600 seconds. After taking the whole hours away, what is left is less than an hour. Do the same for minutes.
